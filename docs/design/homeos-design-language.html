Superseded · 2026-08-01

0% GPU at idle is the hard floor — this study is a frozen record of the design language as it shipped under the old law; it is not being rewritten. The figure under “Atmosphere over decoration” and the “everything else — 0% GPU” row of the motion budget describe the bar the Pi 4 was held to, and are left as they stand.

New work follows DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §0 law 1 — never move for a reason the room can’t see — and the §5.4 budget: quiescent ambient ≤ 8%, live ambient ≤ 25% sustained, peak episode ≤ 35%. Motion may now be continuous and may live on the resting surface, provided it reports a cause outside the screen. “0% at rest” was not relaxed but repealed: it is measurably false in daylight (docs/audit/HOST-BASELINES.md, Correction 3).

The signature

Light is the material. The weather is the palette.

Colour doesn't come from UI chrome — it comes from the sky. The same Glance moment wears the light of the hour and the weather outside, a wash that settles over 60 seconds and never loops. The dashboard becomes another light source in the room.

The rules

Ten principles, each with a rule it can be held to

A design language is a philosophy, not a palette. Every component must justify itself against these — and each principle resolves to a concrete rule tied to a real token, so "calm" is checkable, not claimed.

PrincipleThe concrete rule it becomes
Silence over noiseAmbient shows ≤ 6 words. A candidate below its band stays silent — shouldSpeak() is the one gate, and its default answer is no.
Light over colourColour comes from weather light — the atmo-* wash — never UI chrome. The one warm hue --warm is reserved for sun and lightning only.
Atmosphere over decorationOne resting wash that settles over --atmo-settle (60s) and comes to rest. No loops — 0% GPU at idle is the hard floor.
Meaning over informationExactly one hero may dominate. score = importance × urgency × confidence; presence sets the floor that lets almost everything lose.
Motion over animationtransform / opacity only, and every motion names a reason. Ambient is effectively still — a slow settle, never a marquee.
Presence over interactionNo touch. The mode is chosen for you by where you are and how long you dwell — zero onboarding, zero navigation.
Space over panelsGlass dissolves in Ambient & Glance and earns its edges only in Lean-in. Empty space is the correct state, not a bug to fill.
Restraint over featuresOne voice: every line passes personality.phrase(). Delight fires ≤ 2–3× a year on a hard, persisted budget.
Memory over mediaA photo can carry a memory the house holds. Tender memories are ambient-only and never captioned — gentleness is code, not hope.
Rhythm over layoutThe day's light and the learned routine set tempo — houseModel tempo, not a fixed grid. The room reads a rushed morning differently from a slow Sunday.

Motion & stillness

How information breathes — and where nothing happens

Timing is a manner, shared by every transition from one authority (personality.timing()). Stillness is designed as deliberately as motion.

Timing tokens

  • atmosphere60000ms · easeThe weather wash settling to rest. Slow enough to be ignorable.
  • hero700ms · cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)One thing arriving or replacing another. A calm exchange, not a snap.
  • arrival550ms · cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)A welcome-home card easing in — a touch of overshoot, then settle.

The reduction

What HomeOS removes that a dashboard shows

"Weniger, aber besser." The design isn't what got added — it's what got taken away, and each cut earns its place.

Navigation, tabs, the click-cycle
Presence decides the one job at any moment. A passer-by carrying groceries has no time to navigate.
The grid of always-on widgets
One scored hero. Every "might be useful" tile was cognitive tax paid a hundred times a day.
Permanent labels for absent values
A panel with nothing to say is absent, not blank. Empty space is the ambient default.
Decorative motion
Any continuous animation pins a CPU core on the Pi. Motion now means a state change, nothing else.
Notifications that nag
Silence is the default and delight is budgeted. The house says a thing once, plainly, or not at all.
Pure white, hard chrome
--ink is a soft white-blue; colour is borrowed from the weather, not painted on.

Verify, don't claim

Calm is measurable

A wall display lives at a distance no phone designer plans for. These are the targets a mockup has to hit before it can ship onto the wall — on a 32-inch 1080p panel.

≤ 6words

The one rule

Show the right thing at the right moment — and nothing at all, beautifully, the rest of the time.

A visitor should almost forget there's software involved. Not a hotel-lobby aesthetic borrowed as decoration — the same discipline: presence, restraint, and light.
