The one thing always on the wall

In Mode 0 almost everything disappears. The clock is what stays — the single point of light the room keeps. So it has a harder brief than any hero: legible if you glance, but never bright enough to light the room at 3 a.m., and never burning a shape into the panel over the years it's on.

The face

Big, condensed, and deliberately dim

Jumbo Barlow Condensed numerals in tabular figures, washed in the night accent. Large enough to read across a dark room; dim enough to be furniture. It carries the day beneath it in a single quiet line.

It dims with the sky, not a schedule

The brief within the brief

Bright enough to read. Dim enough to sleep beside.

Every other surface can appear and leave. The clock is the one that stays all night, so its whole craft is knowing exactly how little light to give — and giving it steadily, wherever the digits happen to be drifting.
