HomeOS · Component Study · Arrival

The one moment it speaks to you

Everywhere else the house is careful, ambient, addressed to no one. The arrival card is the exception it earns: a genuine away→home crossing, met by name, for fifteen seconds, then gone. It's the warmest surface in the system — which is exactly why it has to stay disciplined. A warmth, never a fuss.

The moment

Welcome home, by name — then it lets you go

It wakes the screen, greets the person who just walked in, hands them only what's still ahead tonight, speaks it once aloud, and drains a quiet countdown before dismissing itself. No dismiss button, no lingering — the card knows it's a guest.

The discipline

The warmest thing the house does is also the briefest.

Fifteen seconds, by name, then it recedes and the room goes quiet again. Warmth you can rely on precisely because it never overstays — the opposite of a notification that waits to be acknowledged.
